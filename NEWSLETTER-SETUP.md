# SageWise Email Signup

The “Let's Be Friends” form in the Leadership/About section is a front-end demo only. It validates an email address and explains that enrollment is not connected; it does not store or send submitted addresses.

Before launch, connect the form to an approved email-list provider. Configure its endpoint and required consent/unsubscribe behavior, then replace the demo submit handler in `main.js` with the provider integration. Do not collect or transmit addresses until that integration and its privacy notice are in place.

The signup copy highlights early company news, product milestones, and occasional insights into reliable AI and knowledge systems.
